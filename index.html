01 — Directions

Three genuinely different designs

Each concept changes the grid, typography, palette, imagery treatment, and interaction — not just the colours. Open any direction to walk through its Landing and About views. Every view runs on the supplied footage and verified source material only.

A / 01

Foundation

Editorial · Mineral · Institutional credibility

The association as a credible institution. A documentary, mineral-roots design that treats the aggregates story like a printed brief: authoritative, measured, built to be trusted by industry, government, and members alike.

Grid
Fixed modular column grid, formal margins, ruled sections — the page reads like a well-set document.
Type
Serif display headlines, small-caps section labels, dense documentary body copy.
Palette
Stone and cement neutrals, deep graphite, a single oxide accent.
Imagery
The production journey as archival plates — captioned, framed, authoritative.
Interaction
Deliberate and quiet; hierarchy carries the page rather than motion.
  • Highest institutional gravity — strongest fit for governance and regulatory audiences.
  • Documentary treatment of the production footage.
  • Calm, formal, low-friction reading experience.

B / 02

Living Landscapes

Cinematic · Biodiversity · Responsible quarrying

Aggregates and habitat, told cinematically. The most emotive direction: full-bleed landscape frames, birds and water as heroes, and a measured environmental story — quarry landscapes, responsibly managed, can support valuable habitats.

Grid
Asymmetric editorial grid with full-bleed landscape frames; captions run like field notes.
Type
Oversized display type paired with a humanist sans for body text.
Palette
Quarry-water greens and teals, warm sand, deep forest shadow.
Imagery
Nesting banks, water, and wildlife as hero frames; footage integrated as story.
Interaction
Immersive scroll rhythm with controlled motion and full player controls.
  • Strongest departure from the current site — unmistakably new.
  • Footage-forward storytelling; the supplied films carry the emotion.
  • Biodiversity framed carefully — no universal green claims.

A modern hub for a nationwide membership. The association as an active national ecosystem — members, regions, knowledge, and events organised with clear wayfinding, and membership pathways front and centre.

02 — Criteria

What will decide between them

Eight criteria, drawn from the verified brief, legal pack, media review, and implementation audit. The recommendation below weighs all eight rather than treating visual impact as the only test.

  1. Differentiation from the current site

    Structural, typographic, and visual departure — not a colour swap. Each concept must feel like a new organisation.

  2. Fit with verified source material

    How well the design carries the legal-pack facts, repository audit, and video evidence — and how safely it frames the biodiversity story without universal claims.

  3. Membership action clarity

    The existing apply / review / payment pathways survive the redesign and stay findable in a simplified top-level structure.

  4. Institutional credibility

    Appropriate gravity for a non-profit national association addressing industry, government, and members.

  5. Storytelling with the supplied footage

    The three films earn their place on both Landing and About views — with captions, text equivalents, and motion controls.

  6. Accessibility and responsiveness

    Semantic landmarks, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and clean layouts from 375px to 1440px with no horizontal overflow.

  7. Implementation fit

    How naturally each concept maps onto the existing Next.js + Tailwind repository and its component model.

  8. Placeholder discipline

    No invented partners, awards, testimonials, statistics, or membership counts anywhere in the package — only verified facts and clearly labelled placeholders.

The three directions meet the same content and access requirements. Their main trade-off is strategic: institutional gravity, cinematic public storytelling, or member-pathway clarity.

04 — Verdict

Recommendation

Recommended — Living Landscapes

Choose Living Landscapes as the lead direction. It makes the supplied films useful, gives AMSMA a distinct public identity, and explains environmental responsibility with the cautious, site-specific language required by the evidence. Borrow Foundation’s disciplined governance typography and National Network’s persistent membership route during implementation.

Input 1 — Content fit
Which direction carries the brief’s messaging hierarchy and navigation with least friction.
Input 2 — Differentiation
How much departure from the current site the owner wants, and which audience the site leads with.
Input 3 — Story safety
How confidently the biodiversity narrative can be voiced — verified facts first, universal claims never.
Input 4 — Membership goals
Whether growth, credibility, or member services dominate the next phase of the association.
Input 5 — Implementation cost
Component mapping and build effort inside the existing Next.js + Tailwind repository.

Why this direction leads Living Landscapes offers the strongest visual departure and the most natural home for the supplied footage. It is recommended, while Foundation carries the highest institutional gravity for governance and regulatory audiences. Living Landscapes wins because it combines the clearest public story, best use of supplied media, and strongest differentiation while keeping claims within evidence.

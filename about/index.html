About AMSMA

A shared platform for knowledge, responsibility and progress.

The Aggregate & M sand Manufacturers Association serves India’s aggregate and M sand sector. Its purpose connects industry development with education, public benefit and environmental responsibility.

Mission

Why the Association exists.

AMSMA is a forum for technical exchange, education, responsible practice and constructive policy engagement. Its governing objects cover research, training, standards, ethics, technology, cooperation and environmental protection.

The Association uses these objects to guide its programmes, partnerships and representation of the sector.

Production in brief

A connected four-step sequence.

Aggregate production moves through extraction, crushing, conveying and screening before the finished material is placed in stockpiles.

Step 01

Extract

Machinery removes and loads rock.

Step 02

Crush

Equipment reduces the rock.

Step 03

Convey and screen

Material moves through visible screening stages.

Step 04

Stockpile

Finished aggregate is placed in stockpiles.

Governance

Member-led and non-profit.

The governing records place management and administration with a Managing Committee. They provide for meetings, voting, accounts and audit. The Memorandum directs income and property to the Association’s aims rather than distribution as profit to members.

The formation resolution dated 26 May 2026 names eight Managing Committee members. The Committee page presents the current roster.

About AMSMA

A shared platform for knowledge, responsibility and progress.

The Aggregate & M sand Manufacturers Association was formed for registration with an area of operation extending across India. Its objects connect sector development with education, public benefit and environmental responsibility.

Mission

Why the Association exists.

AMSMA was created as a forum for technical exchange, education, responsible practice and constructive policy engagement. Its governing objects cover research, training, standards, ethics, technology, cooperation and environmental protection.

These objects state what the Association is set up to advance. They do not, by themselves, prove that a programme has been delivered or that a result has been achieved.

Governance

Member-led and non-profit.

The governing records place management and administration with a Managing Committee. They provide for meetings, voting, accounts and audit. The Memorandum directs income and property to the Association’s aims rather than distribution as profit to members.

The formation resolution records a meeting on 26 May 2026 and names eight first Managing Committee members. Review the dedicated Committee page for the evidence-based roster.

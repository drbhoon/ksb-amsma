01 — Our workThe production journey

From pit to product: how aggregate is made

Aggregates and manufactured sand are the mineral base of roads, rail, housing, and water infrastructure. AMSMA's work begins with how that material is produced — and how production landscapes are planned and cared for.

I. Excavation & loading Material is excavated from the quarry face and loaded for processing.
II. Crushing Rock is reduced in stages by crushers to target sizes.
III. Conveyor transport Crushed material moves between stages on conveyor lines.
IV. Screening & grading Screens separate material by size and grade.
V. Stockpiling Graded product is stockpiled, ready for dispatch to works.

Responsible practice includes attention to habitat

Quarry landforms can sometimes provide exposed banks, water bodies and vegetated ledges that wildlife can use. Ecological value depends on local conditions, species, operations and long-term management. The illustrative footage does not show an identified AMSMA site.

About the association

A body for the industry, serving the wider community.

AMSMA — the Aggregate & M sand Manufacturers Association — is a not-for-profit association of aggregate and M-sand manufacturers, formed in 2026 to advance technical knowledge and research, education, policy representation, high standards, environmental stewardship, sustainable quarrying, and ethics, with an area of operation across all of India.

Income and property are applied solely to the association's aims; no profit is distributed to members.

01 — AimsStated objectives

What the association exists to do

The association's stated objectives, as set out in its formation documents, span knowledge, education, representation, standards, and stewardship.

01 Service to society To benefit society and the wider community, ahead of any private interest.
02 Knowledge & research To develop and share technical knowledge and research for the industry.
03 Education & training To organise education and training in responsible production.
04 Policy representation To represent the industry before policy and regulatory bodies.
05 Standards & ethics To promote high standards of materials, production, and professional conduct.
06 Environmental stewardship To advance environmental stewardship and sustainable quarrying.
07 Information & innovation To collect and share statistics and information, and to encourage technology and innovation.
08 National development To contribute to national infrastructure and economic development, and to cooperate internationally in knowledge exchange.

02 — MembershipFour categories

Join the association

Membership is open to eligible manufacturers and allied organisations, with categories for suppliers, institutions, and individuals of distinction. Members participate in association meetings and educational activities, and receive information and advice from the association.

A Ordinary Aggregate and M-sand manufacturers meeting the eligibility criteria — a minimum crushing capacity of 50,000 tonnes per month, per the current rules.
B Associate The Rules list consultants, equipment suppliers, transporters, institutions and individuals; individual eligibility is unresolved and needs confirmation.
C Honorary An eminent person may be invited by a two-thirds Managing Committee vote; this category has no vote or right to hold office.
D Institutional Research bodies, industry chambers and academic institutions; fee coverage needs confirmation beyond educational institutions.

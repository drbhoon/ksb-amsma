Extraction

Rock is extracted at member quarry sites, with overburden and land handled responsibly.

Crushing

Oversized rock is reduced in crushers to the sizes construction needs.

Screening & grading

Material is screened and graded, then washed and checked for consistency.

Stockpiling

Finished aggregate is stockpiled and dispatched to infrastructure projects across the country.

Who we are

The story of the association

The formation records state that members met in Mumbai on 26 May 2026 and formed the Association for registration. Its area of operation extends across India, and its registered office is recorded in Thane, Maharashtra. No registration certificate was supplied.

The Memorandum states a non-profit character. Its income and property are used only for its stated aims; no part is distributed to members as profit. Everything it does exists for the industry's common benefit: knowledge and research, policy engagement, environmental protection, sustainable development, education and training, standards and ethics, technology, and international cooperation.

Membership is by application, subject to the detailed category rules and Managing Committee review. The supplied Rules contain an unresolved conflict about individual Associate eligibility.

“An all-India scope for knowledge, responsibility and constructive engagement.”

Why it matters. Aggregates and M-sand are the raw materials of India's infrastructure. The Association seeks to advance knowledge, standards and responsible practice for public benefit. Activity and outcome claims require evidence before launch.

Objectives

What the association stands for

The objectives below are drawn from the association's memorandum of association — the purposes its income and property exist to serve.

01

Policy & representation

Engage with policy and regulation on behalf of the aggregate and M-sand industry, and support national infrastructure and economic development.

02

Environmental protection

Promote environmental protection across extraction, processing and site restoration.

03

Sustainable development

Advance sustainable development in the industry — balancing production, land use and the communities that live with quarries.

04

Knowledge & research

Exchange technical information, research and best practices across the industry and with allied sectors.

05

Education & training

Organise seminars, workshops and training programmes that build capability across the network.

06

Standards & ethics

Uphold professional standards, ethics and responsible conduct among members.

07

Technology & innovation

Encourage technology, innovation and modernisation in aggregate production and M-sand manufacturing.

08

Data & international ties

Maintain and share industry statistics and information among members, and cooperate with international bodies such as GAIN and peer associations.

Membership

Categories, eligibility & rights

Membership is for eligible legal entities; individuals are generally excluded, with honorary membership as the exception. Each category below reflects the association's rules.

ORD

Ordinary

For aggregate and M-sand producer companies.

Eligibility
Producer companies with crushing capacity of 50,000 metric tonnes per month or more.
Rights
Participation in general meetings and educational programmes; information and advice from the association.
ASS

Associate

For suppliers, OEMs and service providers allied to the industry.

Eligibility
Companies supplying equipment, technology or services to aggregate and M-sand producers.
Rights
Participation in the association's meetings and programmes; information and advice on industry matters.
INS

Institutional

For educational and research institutions.

Eligibility
Universities, research bodies and training institutions engaged with construction materials and allied fields.
Rights
Participation in seminars, workshops and training; access to the association's information and advice.
HON

Honorary

An eminent person may be invited by a two-thirds Managing Committee vote.

Eligibility
Recognised individuals; the exception to the general rule that membership is for companies.
Rights
Participation in the association's meetings and programmes, as extended by the association.

# AMSMA — Quarry Strata identity

## Selected direction

**Quarry Strata — Option 3** is the approved AMSMA identity mark. This folder contains the genuine vector-path reproduction of the selected mark rather than a reinterpretation of it.

The master artwork is `AMSMA_QuarryStrata_Option3_Primary.svg`. It contains six filled vector paths and no embedded raster imagery. The production trace removes only pixel stair-stepping inherited from the original concept image while retaining the selected silhouette, cuts, overlaps and proportions.

## Design rationale

The mark is a geometric **A** constructed from stepped quarry strata. Its layered cuts reference quarry benches, aggregate gradation and the successive stages through which raw stone becomes a manufactured construction material. The geometry is deliberately structural rather than illustrative: it should read first as an authoritative association emblem and then reveal its industry reference on closer inspection.

The identity is intended to feel **institutional and industrial**, not like an app icon or technology-startup mark. The broad triangular mass gives it stability, while the stepped horizontal strata give it a specific relationship to quarrying and aggregate manufacture.

### Colour system

- **Quarry Green — `#273D33`**  
  Used for the principal strata. The dark mineral green gives the mark institutional weight while connecting to responsible land stewardship and the industry's sustainability agenda.

- **Earth Brown — `#8E5F30`**  
  Used for the exposed lower-right faces. It references quarried earth, stone and mineral material and creates a clear visual distinction between the structural strata and the material face.

The two colours are intentionally muted rather than bright. This allows the identity to work on official documents, certificates, event material, signage, digital interfaces and formal industry communications without feeling promotional or consumer-oriented.

## Artwork structure

The master mark consists of six independent vector shapes:

- 3 Quarry Green strata
- 3 Earth Brown faces

There are no gradients, strokes, effects, masks, fonts or embedded images in the primary SVG. This makes the asset appropriate for scaling, signage, vinyl cutting, screen printing, embroidery preparation and monochrome reproduction.

## Usage principles

1. **Do not redraw or alter the strata geometry.** The primary SVG is the geometry master.
2. **Do not change the relationship between green and brown shapes** in the standard two-colour mark.
3. **Do not add gradients, shadows, outlines or rounded containers.** The mark is intended to remain flat and architectural.
4. Use the **two-colour primary version** on light, neutral backgrounds whenever possible.
5. Use the **reversed white version** where a dark background makes the primary colours unsuitable.
6. Use the **monochrome version** for black-only printing, stamps, photocopying and other constrained production methods.
7. Preserve generous clear space around the mark. As a practical rule, keep surrounding graphics and type at least the width of the top quarry-strata apex away from the artwork.
8. The standalone mark may be used for social avatars, favicons and compact identifiers, but formal AMSMA communications should pair it with the AMSMA name/wordmark once that lockup is finalised.

## Production note

The original selected concept was raster artwork. This vector was traced directly from that approved Option 3 mark. The production master smooths only raster-induced stair-stepping; it is not a new logo concept.

Membership

Join a community shaping India’s aggregate industry.

Membership is open to eligible businesses, institutions and individuals engaged in the aggregate and M sand sector. Categories, eligibility and subscriptions are governed by Rule 4 of the Association’s Rules & Regulations.

How we use application data

The application asks for identity, contact, organisation, PAN, supporting-document, proposer and seconder details. AMSMA uses this information only to assess and process membership, communicate with the applicant, collect an approved subscription and maintain membership records.

The form requires a separate privacy-consent confirmation before submission. Read the Privacy and Data Retention Notice before you continue.

How it works

Four steps to active membership.

01

Submit the application

Provide the category, organisation details, PAN, address, authorised signatory, a company-proof link, and two existing members as proposer and seconder.

02

Committee review

The Managing Committee reviews the application through secure links. Admission requires approval from six of the eight committee members.

03

Pay after approval

Approved applicants receive a secure payment link for the correct annual subscription. The link is valid for 14 days.

04

Activate membership

After verified payment, the applicant is entered in the Register of Members and receives an AMSMA member number.

Ready to apply?

Prepare the required details.

Have the organisation’s registration details, PAN, registered address, signatory information and a shareable company-proof document link ready.

You will also select two different committee members as proposer and seconder. Their email addresses are added automatically and remain masked.
